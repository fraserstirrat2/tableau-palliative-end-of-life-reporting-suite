# Service Utilisation — Validation and Development

This page will document the QA approach and evidence-backed development history for the Service Utilisation dashboard.

## Validation principle

The final case study should demonstrate that Tableau reproduces the governed service-utilisation output accurately across displayed years, measures, filters and reporting states.

## Checks to document

- source-to-dashboard reconciliation;
- totals/rates/percentages where applicable;
- filters/parameters/actions;
- agreement between related views;
- time/geography ordering;
- refresh checks;
- labels, legends, tooltips and information text;
- navigation/help behaviour;
- governance/disclosure review.

## Development history

Add only retained examples of stakeholder feedback, layout/control changes, terminology refinements, QA fixes or refresh issues that are supported by evidence.

A technical reviewer should understand the reporting question, contribution boundary, Tableau structure, QA approach, iterative development and public-safety safeguards without needing production access.
