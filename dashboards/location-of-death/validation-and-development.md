# Location of Death — Validation and Development

This page will document the QA approach and evidence-backed development history for the Location of Death dashboard.

## Validation principle

The final case study should demonstrate that Tableau reproduces the governed location-of-death output accurately across displayed years, categories, filters and reporting states.

## Checks to document

- source-to-dashboard reconciliation;
- totals/percentages/rates where applicable;
- category mapping;
- filters/parameters/actions;
- agreement between related views;
- time/geography ordering;
- refresh checks;
- labels, legends, tooltips and information text;
- navigation/help behaviour;
- governance/disclosure review.
